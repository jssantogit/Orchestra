# Jev Lab

Jev is an Orchestra Lab. Its authority is explicitly `NONE`.

Jev may perform bounded retrieval, ranking, counterfactual analysis, evaluation, and storage of lab-scoped results. Jev output is advisory: it cannot accept work, grant capabilities, claim Work Leases, mutate product state, or promote itself into acceptance/evidence authority.

Any compatibility launcher must preserve the zero-authority contract in `labs/lab-authority.mjs`.
